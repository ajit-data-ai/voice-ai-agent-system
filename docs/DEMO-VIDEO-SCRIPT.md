# 🎥 90-Second Demo Video — Complete Script

> **Goal:** Convert prospect → demo call in 90 seconds. Show, don't tell.
>
> **Format:** Screen recording + phone call audio overlay. No talking head needed.
> **Total runtime:** 90 seconds (do not exceed)
> **Upload to:** YouTube (unlisted) + embed link in README + use in Upwork proposals

---

## 🎬 Production Setup (Before You Record)

| Item | Setup |
|------|-------|
| **Screen recorder** | Loom, OBS, or QuickTime (Mac) — record full screen at 1080p |
| **Phone recording** | Use a second phone to record your own phone screen, OR enable iPhone Call Recording / Android Cube Recorder |
| **Mic** | Headphones with mic — keeps phone audio clean |
| **Background music** | Free upbeat track from YouTube Audio Library (Lo-fi or "Corporate Inspiration") at 15% volume |
| **Calendar visible** | Have Google Calendar open in browser, zoomed in on tomorrow's date |
| **SMS visible** | Have iMessage/Messages app open on screen too |

## 🎞️ Scene-by-Scene Script

### 🟦 SCENE 1 — Hook (0:00 – 0:08)
**Visual:** Black screen with bold white text typing on, fast:

```
The average small business loses $126,000/year
to missed calls.
85% of callers who don't reach someone... never call back.
```

**Audio:** Just background music, no voice yet.
**Transition:** Quick swipe to scene 2.

---

### 🟩 SCENE 2 — Reveal (0:08 – 0:13)
**Visual:** Full screen text on solid blue background:

```
What if every call got answered —
even at 2am?
```

**Hold for 3 seconds.** Cut to scene 3.

---

### 🟨 SCENE 3 — The Call Begins (0:13 – 0:30)
**Visual:** Split screen
- **Left half:** Phone calling screen (your iPhone/Android dialing)
- **Right half:** Live Google Calendar showing tomorrow's slots

**Audio:** Phone ringing → answer click → Sophie speaks:

> 🤖 *"Thank you for calling Smile Perfect Dental, this is Sophie! How can I help you today?"*

**You (caller):**
> 👤 *"Hi, I'd like to book a teeth cleaning for tomorrow afternoon."*

**Sophie:**
> 🤖 *"I'd be happy to help! Are you a new patient with us, or have you visited before?"*

**You:**
> 👤 *"I'm a new patient."*

**Sophie:**
> 🤖 *"Perfect! Can I get your full name and best phone number?"*

**You:**
> 👤 *"John Smith. 555-123-4567."*

---

### 🟧 SCENE 4 — The Magic Moment (0:30 – 0:55)
**Visual:** Zoom into Google Calendar on right side as Sophie speaks. Highlight the open 2:00 PM slot with a soft glow effect.

**Sophie (checking calendar):**
> 🤖 *"Got it. Just one moment while I check availability for you... I have a 2:00 PM opening tomorrow with Dr. Smith — does that work?"*

**You:**
> 👤 *"Yes, that's perfect."*

**Sophie:**
> 🤖 *"Just to confirm — John Smith, teeth cleaning, tomorrow at 2:00 PM with Dr. Smith. We'll text confirmation to 555-123-4567. Sound right?"*

**You:**
> 👤 *"Yes."*

**Sophie:**
> 🤖 *"You're all set! See you tomorrow at 2 PM!"*

---

### 🟪 SCENE 5 — The Proof (0:55 – 1:15)
**Visual sequence (5s each):**

1. **Calendar event appears** — animate the new event "[NEW] Dental Cleaning — John Smith" populating into the 2 PM slot. Add a green checkmark.

2. **SMS arrives** — iPhone notification slides in from top showing:
   ```
   Hi John! ✅ Your appointment is confirmed.
   📅 Tomorrow at 2:00 PM
   🦷 Dental Cleaning
   📍 Smile Perfect Dental
   ```

3. **Email backup** — Gmail icon shows "1 new" with the same content visible.

**No voiceover.** Let the visuals speak. Background music swells slightly.

---

### 🟥 SCENE 6 — Call to Action (1:15 – 1:30)
**Visual:** Bold text on dark background:

```
✅ Booked in 47 seconds
✅ Zero staff time used
✅ Works 24/7 — even at 2am

Want this for your business?
```

**Final frame (hold 4 seconds):**

```
voice-ai-agent.com  |  Call (XXX) XXX-XXXX
```

(Your contact info / website / phone number)

**Music fades out.** End.

## 🎯 What Makes This Video Convert

| Element | Why it works |
|---------|-------------|
| **Hook with a number** | "$126,000/year lost" — concrete, painful, fact-based |
| **No talking head** | Audience never feels lectured — they see results |
| **Split-screen during call** | Proves the calendar updates in real-time (not faked) |
| **SMS visible on real phone** | Trust signal — "this actually integrates with my phone" |
| **47-second metric** | Specific = memorable. "Fast" is forgettable. |
| **24/7 pitch at end** | The single biggest pain point service businesses have |
| **No "AI does everything" hype** | Just shows it working. Lets the prospect imagine their use case. |

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Don't add captions explaining the AI — let it speak for itself
- ❌ Don't show your face talking — kills the "this is real" effect
- ❌ Don't speed up the phone call — natural pacing = credibility
- ❌ Don't use stock footage — the entire point is showing YOUR working system
- ❌ Don't put YOUR logo before the demo — prospect should see VALUE before BRAND
- ❌ Don't make it longer than 90 seconds — drop-off rate spikes after 60s
